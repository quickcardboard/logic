How to play

  • Everyone stood on a different place.
  • Right next to means joined by one path. Two paths apart and far (3 or more) count the fewest paths.
  • A dead end has one path. A crossroads has three or more.
  • The evidence is always true. Honest people tell the truth.
  • The Fibber's statements are all false.
  • The Muddler gets exactly one statement wrong.
  • Pick a person, then tap a place to pencil them in as a maybe, or press and hold to place them. You can also drag a person onto the map.
  • Tap a statement to tick it ✓ or cross it ✗ while you think.
