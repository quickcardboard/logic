Who Was Where?

Place every suspect on the map. Find the Fibber.

Tap a suspect card, then tap a spot to put them there. Tap a spot again to clear it.

How to play
  • Each suspect stood on a different spot.
  • Right next to means joined by one path. Two paths apart and far (3 or more) count the fewest paths between two spots.
  • Dead end means one path leaves that spot. A crossroads has three or more.
  • Honest suspects tell the truth. The Fibber's statements are all false.
  • The Muddler is honest but forgetful: exactly one of their statements is false.
  • The evidence is always true.
  • Tap a statement to mark it true ✓ or false ✗ as you work. That's just for your notes.
