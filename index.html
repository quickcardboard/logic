Who Was Where?

Place every suspect on the map. Find the Fibber.

Select a suspect card. Tap a spot to pencil them in as a maybe. Press and hold a spot to place them there.

How to play
  • Each suspect stood on a different spot.
  • Right next to means joined by one path. Two paths apart and far (3 or more) count the fewest paths between two spots.
  • Dead end means one path leaves that spot. A crossroads has three or more.
  • Honest suspects tell the truth.
  • The Fibber's statements are all false.
  • The Muddler is honest but forgetful: exactly one of their statements is false.
  • The evidence is always true.
  • Where to start: look for statements about a person's own spot, like "I was at a dead end". Pencil in the few spots that fit, then use statements about other people.
  • Select a suspect, then tap a spot to pencil them in as a maybe, or press and hold to place them. Hold a placed suspect's spot to pick them back up.
  • Tap a statement to mark it true ✓ or false ✗ as you work. That's just for your notes.
